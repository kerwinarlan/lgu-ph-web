# Philippine LGU Website Compliance Framework

This document outlines legal and technical standards for Philippine Local Government Unit (LGU) websites.

## 1. DILG Full Disclosure Policy (FDP)

The Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) requires LGUs to publish financial documents.
Reference: DILG Memorandum Circular No. 2013-140.

### Required Documents
* Annual Executive Budget Report
* Special Education Fund (SEF) Utilization Report
* 20% Component Fund Utilization Report
* Annual Procurement Plan (APP)
* Bid Results on Civil Works, Goods, and Consulting Services
* Unliquidated Cash Advances Report

## 2. ARTA Citizen's Charter (RA 11032)

The Anti-Red Tape Authority (ARTA) mandates public service disclosure under Republic Act 11032.

### Checklist
* List all public service workflows clearly.
* State responsible offices and officers.
* State required documents per service.
* State exact processing times and fees.
* Provide downloadable official forms.

## 3. DICT e-Government Web Standards

The Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) sets website standards.

### Requirements
* Mobile-responsive interface for low-bandwidth mobile networks.
* Accessibility compliance with WCAG 2.1 Level AA.
* Clear display of official Philippine LGU seals.
* HTTPS encryption for data protection.
