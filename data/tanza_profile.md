# Profile: Municipality of Tanza, Cavite

Official municipal profile and reference dataset extracted from Philippine Census and MGB/DILG records.

## General Information

* **Official Name:** Municipality of Tanza (*Bayan ng Tanza*)
* **Former Name:** Santa Cruz de Malabon
* **Honorific / Nickname:** Heart of the Cavite Coast
* **Motto:** *Serbisyo sa Mamamayan, Malasakit Para sa Bayan*
* **Province:** Cavite
* **Region:** Region IV-A (CALABARZON)
* **Congressional District:** 7th District of Cavite
* **PSGC Code:** `0402120000`
* **ZIP Code:** 4108
* **Income Classification:** 1st Class Municipal Income Class

## Demographics & Geography

* **Population (2024 Census):** 339,308 inhabitants
* **Population Density:** 4,332 / km² (11,220 / sq mi)
* **Total Households:** 79,243
* **Land Area:** 78.33 km² (30.24 sq mi)
* **Barangay Count:** 41 Barangays
* **Topography:** Coastal plain along Manila Bay, elevation 0 to 642 meters.

## Historical Significance

1. **Oath-Taking of Emilio Aguinaldo (1897):**
   Emilio Aguinaldo took his oath as President of the Philippine Revolutionary Government at the Holy Cross Parish in Tanza following the Tejeros Convention.
2. **Birthplace of Felipe G. Calderón:**
   Tanza is the birthplace of Felipe Calderón, author of the 1899 Malolos Constitution.
