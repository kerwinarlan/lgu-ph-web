The Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO) advises all residents in low-lying and slope-adjacent barangays to monitor local weather updates.

## Key Instructions

1. Secure light roof materials and clear drainage pathways near residences.
2. Keep emergency hotlines saved on mobile phones.
3. Coordinate with local Barangay Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Committees (BDRRMC) for evacuation notices.
